# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Mathcraft to be a project where people can take part without fear of harassment, whatever
their background, experience, identity or way of working. Good engineering needs honest review,
and honest review needs respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Rust, to maths software, or to
  open source.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Say what is wrong and how to fix it.
- Give credit, and say when a result or a fix came from someone else.
- Keep discussions on the project's topics, and keep private material out of them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind, in any space of the project.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Posting copyrighted material that the poster has no right to share, including reference
  material from proprietary software, in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Deliberately misleading others about results or about who wrote what.

## Scope

This applies in the project's issues, pull requests, discussions, chat channels, and any other
space run by the project, and when a person represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the project maintainers through the contact given in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (for conduct matters, use the same private route and say that it is a
conduct report). Reports are read by maintainers, and kept confidential as far as the situation
allows. Maintainers will respond, decide what action is needed, and tell the reporter the outcome
where appropriate.

## Consequences

Maintainers may ask someone to change their behaviour, remove content, or stop taking part for a
period or permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

## Attribution

This text is written for this project. It follows the general shape of common open-source codes of
conduct, such as the Contributor Covenant, which it does not reproduce.
